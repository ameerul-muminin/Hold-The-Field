No AI-generated content is presented as real data. The sample dataset (`data/haor-2017.json`) is synthetic, including the December 2016 days added for the planting-date choice, and is labelled as such in the game until it is replaced. The same applies to `data/barind-2022.json` (made by `data-pipeline/make_sample_barind.py`; a monsoon followed by an invented dry spell, not the weather of 2022) by real NASA POWER data (launcher > NASA Data > Update NASA data). All textures, 3D models and sounds in the game are generated by code; no third-party art or audio is used. Fonts: DejaVu Sans (free licence, `game/src/main/resources/fonts/DEJAVU_LICENSE.txt`) and Noto Sans Bengali (SIL Open Font License, `game/src/main/resources/fonts/NOTO_BENGALI_LICENSE.txt`, taken from the `@fontsource/noto-sans-bengali` npm package and converted from WOFF to TTF). The app icon in `packaging/icons/` is drawn by a script.
