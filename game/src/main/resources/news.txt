AGROCENE 1.0.0
NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 - Field Shift

WHAT IT IS
You farm rice in the Sunamganj haor. Replay a real season day by day, read the NASA satellite
data, and decide how to adapt: which rice to plant, when to transplant, whether to raise the
embankment, when to harvest. Then see what each choice would have been worth.

NEW IN 1.0
- A realistic 3D farm: physically-based lighting, shadows, an atmospheric sky, flowing water,
  thousands of animated rice plants, weather and thunderstorms.
- Plan the season: short- or long-duration rice, and the transplanting date.
- NASA satellite view with the farm and the upstream hills labelled with real coordinates.
- Scout dashboard with interactive charts of rain, soil wetness, temperature and the water level.
- A debrief that compares you with the farmer who got no warning and tests every plan on the
  same real weather. Save it as a report.
- Talk to Rahim (right-click) and earn advancements.

REAL NASA DATA
The game ships with SAMPLE data so it always starts. Open the "NASA Data" tab and press
"Update NASA data" to download the real 2016-17 season from NASA POWER. You need an internet
connection once; after that the game works offline.

TIPS
- Rain in the Meghalaya hills reaches the haor about two days later.
- Short-duration rice ripens sooner but yields less. You can harvest from 80% maturity.
- Double-tap Space to fly up and see over the embankment. Press M for the satellite view.
